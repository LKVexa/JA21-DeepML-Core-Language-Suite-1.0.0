# Twelve-Week DeepML Core Curriculum

| Week | Theme | Reading | Applied work |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Language identity, policies, and evidence chain | Chapters 1–2 | Trace source to validation for 20 foundation scripts. |
| 2 | Dtypes, shapes, declarations, and broadcasting | Chapters 3–4 | Write and diagnose legal and illegal tensor programs. |
| 3 | Functions, graphs, control, and inference | Chapters 5–6 | Build a typed inference graph and target adapter table. |
| 4 | Training, autodiff, losses, and optimizers | Chapter 7 | Design one deterministic training step and checkpoint state map. |
| 5 | Randomness, replay, and distributed execution | Chapters 8–9 | Define stable random streams and collective ordering. |
| 6 | Checkpoints and mixed precision | Chapters 10–11 | Test compatibility, migration, casting, and tolerances. |
| 7 | Integrated systems and model packages | Chapter 12 | Package train and infer routes with hashes and adapters. |
| 8 | JA21 scene, timeline, and logic bindings | Chapter 13 | Specify two cross-suite adapters and provenance routes. |
| 9 | Compiler pipeline, AST, semantics, and safety | Chapters 14–15 | Implement or model the front half of the compiler. |
| 10 | Optimization, R12, and MCRT | Chapters 16–17 | Prove one optimization and emit evidence records. |
| 11 | Diagnostics, conformance, and certification | Chapters 18–19 | Run a stage-aware negative and holdout test campaign. |
| 12 | 8S profile, performance, and capstone release | Chapters 20–21 | Publish a bounded toolchain with manifest and limitations. |

## Assessment

- 20% weekly laboratories
- 20% diagnostic and negative-example portfolio
- 20% compiler/runtime evidence notebook
- 40% standalone toolchain capstone and conformance report

A passing capstone must disclose unimplemented features and must not present modeled outcomes as native execution evidence.
